Roll a 6 to bring a token out. A 6 gives another roll, but three 6s in a row loses the turn. Land on a rival to send them back to their yard. Each colour has one safe star square that only protects that colour, and all four stars move to new spots after every round. Landing on a dark −2 square moves you back two, capturing anyone you land on. The last square of each home lane bounces you back out onto the board. You need the exact number to reach the centre.
